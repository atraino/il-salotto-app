## Notes

- **6, 7 and 10 each appear three times** at different sizes. One good photo each is enough, but if
  you have two or three of Trieste, send them and I'll vary them so the same image doesn't repeat.
- **1 and 5 are both doorways** and should not be the same photo: one is Trieste and specific, the
  other is any warm Italian doorway.
- **11 and 12 stand in for members' own photos**, so they should look like snapshots, not
  photography. A phone picture is right.
- The archive tiles (13 to 15) are small, so pick images that read at a glance: one strong shape or
  colour, not a busy street scene.

Send them however is easiest, named loosely by subject, and I'll place them.
